Getting Started

Basic Usage

  • Click "Start Speaking" to begin voice interaction
  • Speak clearly into your microphone
  • The AI will respond with voice and text
  • Use "Streaming Mode" for real-time conversation

Sample Phrases

  • "What's my account balance?"
  • "I need help with a transaction"
  • "I think there's fraud on my account"
  • "Can you help me transfer money?"

Troubleshooting

  • Ensure microphone permissions are granted
  • Check your internet connection
  • Verify API key is set in Settings
  • Use Debug Tools for detailed diagnostics
